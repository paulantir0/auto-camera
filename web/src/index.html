Book Scanner

Turn a video of a book into pictures of its pages

Film the book while you turn the pages. Choose the video here, and this page picks one clear picture of every two-page spread, in order.

Your video stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded; all the work happens inside this browser.

or drop a video file here

  • Best in Safari on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, or in Chrome or Edge on a computer that plays HEVC video.
  • A long video takes a few minutes. Keep this page open and the screen on until it finishes.
  • When the pictures are ready you can have the page numbers checked, to see whether any pages are missing.
